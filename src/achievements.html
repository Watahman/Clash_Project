P

Privacy boundary

Personal completion belongs to the player.

The catalog can be public because the account-specific layer is separate. Personal tiers, XP and snapshots stay behind sign-in and are never substituted with invented public data.

  • Public pages show catalog structure, not player names or tags.
  • Sample progress is labelled and cannot be mistaken for a live account.
  • Private achievement data is evaluated only in the authenticated workspace.
