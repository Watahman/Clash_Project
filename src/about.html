Built for the people doing the organising.

ClashPanel is a free, independent Clash of Clans toolkit for leaders and co-leaders who want to plan CWL, follow the week live and keep multiple clans organised without stitching together spreadsheets, notes and separate trackers.

Start for free See the CWL Planner
01 / WHY

One workflow instead of scattered admin.

CWL planning gets complicated quickly when availability, player accounts, clan lineups, live wars and bonus decisions all live in different places. ClashPanel is built to keep those steps connected.

02 / FOCUS

Decision support, not fake automation.

Auto Plan and optimization are meant to remove repetitive work. They preview and explain suggestions so the clan leadership still decides what actually happens.

03 / BUILT AROUND

The parts of clan management that are easy to lose track of.

  • Multi-clan CWL rosters, rotations and seven-day lineups.
  • Live league context, missed attacks, standings and bonus analysis.
  • Clan Families, linked accounts, member roles and availability polls.
  • Historical CWL context and performance information when reliable data is available.
04 / PRINCIPLE

Useful before impressive.

ClashPanel is intentionally built around practical clan workflows. Features should solve a real planning or coordination problem before they earn space in the product.

05 / INDEPENDENT

A fan-made project, not a Supercell product.

ClashPanel is unofficial and independent. Clash of Clans, Supercell and related game content belong to their respective owners and are used subject to the applicable fan-content rules.
