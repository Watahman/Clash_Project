Why it exists

Less copying. More context.

CWL work gets messy when rosters, availability, attacks and clan relationships live in different places. ClashPanel is built to keep those parts connected.

The product idea

Tools that work better together.

Each ClashPanel tool should solve a real task on its own. The bigger advantage comes from reusing the same roster, availability and clan relationships instead of entering them again.

CWL PlannerBuild multi-clan roster plans with player roles, availability and optimization before league week starts.
CWL TrackerFollow live wars, standings, participation, bonus context and previous seasons.
Clan FamiliesKeep members, linked accounts, clans and availability connected around the same workflow.

How the project is maintained

Useful output, visible limits.

ClashPanel is maintained as an independent fan-made project. Features are designed around connected planning, tracking and multi-clan coordination tasks. Recommendations remain reviewable: unknown data stays unknown, demo data is labelled and organisers keep the final decision.

Corrections and support

Report a calculation concern, stale description, accessibility problem or factual error through the public contact form or by email. Include the affected page and enough non-private detail to reproduce the issue. Published documentation and the changelog are updated when a confirmed correction changes what users should expect.

External data limits

Player, clan, war and historical information can depend on the official Clash of Clans API and selected history providers. Data can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. ClashPanel does not treat a missing value as proof of zero activity and does not guarantee planner or prediction outcomes.
