Honest limits

Incomplete history should change the conclusion.

The fixture begins at a defined starting point. Advanced Stats never treats an unavailable value as zero activity.

  • No activity before tracking began is inferred.
  • Army composition can be missing even when an attack event is known.
  • Tracking starts only after a player explicitly opts in and can be paused.
