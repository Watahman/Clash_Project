How to plan CWL when player availability changes

Late availability changes are normal in a seven-day league. A safe response checks the affected day, protects a complete roster and leaves the reason for each move visible.

Written by ClashPanel · Updated 27 August 2026

Guide 03 · 4 August 2026 · ClashPanel

Update a CWL roster when availability changes

The problem. A seven-day plan becomes unreliable when an account changes its availability after lineups were shared.

Workflow

  1. Record availability per account, not only per person; one member may control several accounts with different commitments.
  2. Mark confirmed “no” separately from unknown. Unknown is a risk, not an available player.
  3. After a change, inspect only affected days first and count the remaining active lineup.
  4. Promote an already assigned Reserve before moving a player between clans.
  5. If a move is necessary, verify the source clan remains complete on every day.
  6. Tell affected players what changed and why.

Trade-off

Late changes can make equal appearances impossible. Prioritise a complete legal lineup, then document how the missed opportunity will be handled in a later round or season. Never silently convert an unknown answer into availability: it makes the plan look safer than it is.
