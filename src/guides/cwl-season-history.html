How to analyse CWL history across several seasons

One week is a small and noisy sample. A multi-season view adds context when the comparison keeps roster changes, matchup conditions and missing records visible.

Written by ClashPanel · Updated 27 August 2026

Guide 07 · 4 August 2026 · ClashPanel

Analyse several CWL seasons instead of one week

The problem. One week mixes roster changes, matchup luck and small samples. A multi-season view adds context but can also compare different conditions unfairly.

Workflow

  1. Check data quality and war-detail completeness for every season.
  2. Compare league, finish, record, attack usage, star differential and destruction differential.
  3. Note roster or Town Hall changes before attributing a trend to performance.
  4. Separate recorded league values from defensibly reconstructed movement.
  5. Investigate a repeated direction over several comparable seasons.

Trade-off

More seasons increase context but also increase the chance that roster strength, game balance or provider coverage changed. Keep the comparison descriptive. If a season lacks the details needed for attack usage, show it as unknown rather than silently comparing it with complete seasons.
