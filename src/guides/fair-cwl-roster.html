How to build a fair CWL roster for seven war days

Fair CWL planning starts by defining what the clan is trying to balance. Availability, roster coverage and development goals should be visible before a player is moved into a lineup.

How to build a fair CWL roster

The decision. “Fair” can mean prioritising strength, development or participation. Those goals conflict unless the clan names its priority before assigning players.

Workflow

  1. Separate non-negotiable Core players from Rotation candidates and true Reserves.
  2. Record player availability before assigning roles.
  3. Fill each clan to its required roster size before adding extra depth.
  4. Compare Town Hall coverage, roles and clan expectations before moving a player.
  5. Review the complete distribution and explain any exception before applying the plan.

Mistakes and trade-offs

Do not hide the assignment rule, overfill one clan while another is incomplete, or treat Reserve as a punishment. A stronger clan may receive priority, but name that choice and keep the final decision reviewable. Availability is part of the opportunity calculation: a player who cannot play a day should not be counted as having been benched.

A fair plan is therefore not necessarily an equal number of appearances. It is a plan that uses the available pool, applies the same announced rules and leaves a clear reason for exceptions.
