How to handle missed CWL attacks fairly

A missed attack is a coordination signal, not a complete explanation. Review the final war state and the player’s actual assignment before applying a clan policy.

Written by ClashPanel · Updated 27 August 2026

Guide 05 · 4 August 2026 · ClashPanel

Handle missed attacks fairly

The problem. A missed-attack count can identify a coordination issue, but it does not explain why the attack was missed.

Workflow

  1. Confirm the war has complete member details and the player was actually in that day’s roster.
  2. Wait for the API to reflect the final war state before publishing a count.
  3. Separate a technical or emergency exception from an unexplained miss.
  4. Apply the clan’s stated rule consistently.
  5. Use repeated misses as a planning signal; do not publicly shame someone from one number.

Mistakes and limits

Do not count unknown details as a miss, compare someone who played two rounds with someone who played seven without context, or use the tracker as the final disciplinary decision. A complete record can still lack the reason behind a missed attack, so keep the judgement with the clan leadership.
