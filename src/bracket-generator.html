Tournament flow

From participants to champion.

The planned bracket workflow is deliberately simple: add participants, create the first matchups and advance winners until one remains.

  1. 01

    Add participants

    Enter the players or clans that should take part in the tournament.

  2. 02

    Seed or shuffle

    Keep the supplied order for seeded matchups or shuffle the opening round.

  3. 03

    Advance winners

    Choose each match winner and let the bracket carry that participant into the next round.

Planned feature

Seed it or shuffle it.

Different community tournaments need different starts. The bracket is designed to support ordered seeds as well as a quick randomized draw.

Seeded orderKeep the participant order when the tournament already has rankings or seeds.
Randomized drawShuffle the opening matchups when the event needs a neutral random start.
Clear roundsKeep quarter-finals, semi-finals and the final easy to follow as the bracket progresses.
Start with the right matchups

Planned feature

Follow every winner forward.

The goal is to remove manual bracket bookkeeping: select a match winner and keep the path to the final visible.

Automatic progressionMove a selected winner into the correct next-round slot without rebuilding the bracket.
Simple tournament stateKeep the current round, completed matchups and eventual champion visible in one view.
Import and exportThe planned workflow includes JSON import and export so a bracket can be saved and reopened.
