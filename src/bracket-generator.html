What the bracket generator does

Enter a list of Clash of Clans players or clans, keep the given order for a seeded bracket or shuffle the participants. Selecting a winner automatically advances that participant to the next round.

Key bracket features

  • Seeded knockout brackets
  • Shuffled brackets
  • Automatic round progression
  • JSON import and export

How it works

  1. Add one tournament participant per line.
  2. Generate the bracket in order or shuffle the initial matchups.
  3. Choose each match winner until the bracket produces a champion.
