What it connects

The people, accounts and clans behind your CWL.

Members

Invite the people who help organise your network and give leaders the controls they need.

Player accounts

Connect multiple Clash of Clans accounts to the correct member instead of tracking identities manually.

Linked clans

Keep the clans in your network reusable across the shared ClashPanel workspace.

Availability polls

Collect a separate response for each account and CWL day, close finished polls and remove old ones.

Planner handoff

Load linked accounts and poll results directly into the CWL Planner when roster work begins.

How it works

Set up the network once, reuse it every CWL.

  1. 01

    Create or join

    Start a Clan Family or join one with its invitation code.

  2. 02

    Connect the network

    Add members, link player accounts and connect the clans they organise.

  3. 03

    Collect availability

    Run a CWL poll and load the answers into the Planner.
