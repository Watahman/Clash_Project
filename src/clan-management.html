Manage Multiple Clans

Clan Family keeps related Clash of Clans clans in one reusable group. Leaders can see which clans belong together and use those linked clans in other ClashPanel workflows.

Clan Members and Linked Accounts

Members can link multiple player accounts while leaders manage roles and compare the group with live clan rosters.

  • Multiple linked clans
  • Clan members
  • Linked player accounts
  • Roles and permissions

Player Availability

Create focused availability polls and collect answers per linked account and CWL day. Poll results can feed directly into roster planning instead of being copied from chat.

  • Member availability

How it works

  1. Create a Clan Family or join one with its invitation code.
  2. Add members, link player accounts and connect the clans in your network.
  3. Coordinate availability and reuse the same clan data in CWL planning.
