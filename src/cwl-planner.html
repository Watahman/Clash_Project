One planning flow

From player pool to a clear CWL plan.

Start with the players you already have, add the context that matters and turn one roster into a complete CWL plan.

  1. 01

    Player pool

    Add players by tag, account, Clan Family or spreadsheet and keep the shared player pool in one place.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Mark core, rotation and reserve players, then use each player's CWL-day availability to guide your decisions.

  3. 03

    Review

    Create the plan manually, use Auto Plan for a starting clan distribution or review targeted optimization suggestions.
