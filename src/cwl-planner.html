One planning flow

From player pool to a clear CWL plan.

Start with the players you already have, add the context that matters and turn one roster into a complete CWL plan.

  1. 01

    Load the roster

    Add players by tag, account, Clan Family or spreadsheet and keep the shared player pool in one place.

  2. 02

    Set roles and availability

    Mark core, rotation and reserve players, then distribute them across the clans in your complete roster plan.

  3. 03

    Review and apply

    Resolve every warning, confirm the seven-day coverage and then apply the roster when it is ready.
