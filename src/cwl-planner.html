What the CWL Planner does

ClashPanel turns a player pool into a practical Clan War League plan. Leaders can combine players from linked clans, direct player tags or spreadsheets and decide who is core, rotation or reserve.

Automatic planning builds a complete starting point, while roster optimization proposes smaller improvements without replacing manual control.

Key planning features

  • Multi-clan CWL planning
  • Core, rotation and reserve roles
  • Automatic roster planning
  • Roster optimization
  • Player availability
  • Historical player performance

How it works

  1. Load players and clans from tags, linked accounts, a Clan Family or a spreadsheet.
  2. Build the roster manually or generate a complete CWL plan automatically.
  3. Review roles and optimize the seven daily lineups before the league starts.
