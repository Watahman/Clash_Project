# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clash of Clans clan leaders, co-leaders, and CWL organizers who prepare rosters, coordinate availability, and manage live war operations across desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

ClashPanel turns clan, player, poll, and war data into practical planning workflows. Success means an organizer can make confident roster decisions before CWL, keep the operation understandable during CWL, and reuse the same trusted information across planning and follow-up tools.

## Brand Personality

Focused, professional, and dependable. The interface should feel calm under time pressure and make dense operational data easy to scan without becoming decorative or game-like.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, oversized decorative metrics, heavy glass effects, novelty game UI, and hidden interactions that interfere with core controls. New features should not make already-dense player cards taller or visually noisier.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the organizer's next decision obvious.
2. Reveal supporting detail progressively without obscuring the core roster.
3. Treat unknown data as unknown and communicate confidence honestly.
4. Preserve familiar controls and interaction patterns across Planner and Operation Board.
5. Make every workflow usable with keyboard, pointer, touch, and reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard behavior. Do not rely on color alone, preserve visible focus states, provide clear accessible names, keep touch targets usable, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
